Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • kalyanima.in

Kalyani Ma (“we,” “our,” or “the Archive”) respects your privacy and collects only the minimum information required to deliver The Household Botanical Blueprint and verify partner referral attribution.

1. Information We Collect

  • Email Delivery Opt-In: When you request the Blueprint via email, we collect your first name and email address to deliver the guide through our email service provider (Brevo).
  • WhatsApp Delivery Opt-In: When you initiate a Blueprint request via WhatsApp, your phone number and message text are processed through the Meta WhatsApp Business Cloud API strictly to send your requested Blueprint link.
  • Referral Attribution: If you arrive through an independent partner link, a referral identifier may be stored locally in your browser (localStorage) so your Blueprint PDF reflects the referring partner’s wholesale access link.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information exclusively to deliver your requested educational guide, maintain technical security, and credit referring partners. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal contact details to third-party data brokers.

3. Your Choices & Unsubscribing

Every email includes a one-click unsubscribe link at the bottom. You may unsubscribe at any time, and you may clear local referral attribution anytime by clearing your browser’s site storage.
