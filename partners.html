How Power of 3 Qualifies: Team Volume vs. Organizational Volume

How Power of 3 qualifies (each month): (1) You place your own 100 PV order on LRP (the monthly autoship). (2) Three people you personally placed on your first level each order at least 100 PV. (3) Those three together reach 500 PV or more (for example, 300 + 100 + 100 or 200 + 200 + 100). That’s your 100 plus 500 from your first level, for 600 Team Volume. Anything you order over 100 PV doesn’t count toward the 600, and neither does volume from people below your first three—that deeper volume is Organizational Volume (OV), used for leadership ranks like Elite (3,000 OV) and Premier (5,000 OV). Example: You 100 + first level 200, 200, 100 qualifies; You 300 + first level 100, 100, 100 does not qualify because your first level only reached 300 PV.
