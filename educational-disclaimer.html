1. Not Medical Advice

Nothing on this website, in our audio recordings, or in our downloadable guides constitutes medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or a prescription. Essential oils and plant extracts are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional regarding any medical condition, pregnancy, nursing, or use around infants, children, or pets.

2. Safe Handling & Dilution

Pure steam-distilled and resin-derived essential oils are highly concentrated volatile compounds. Never apply undiluted essential oils directly to sensitive skin, eyes, or mucous membranes, and always perform a small patch test in an appropriate carrier oil prior to household use.
